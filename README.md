## 🕹️ About

EVE Vanguard is a sci-fi extraction-adventure MMOFPS set in New Eden, the universe of EVE Online. Play as an immortal Warclone deployed to hostile planets to raid, scavenge, complete contracts, fight rival players and NPC forces, and extract valuable technology. The game is in pre-release development with ongoing playtests and planned Early Access.

Built for players who enjoy tactical sci-fi shooters, extraction gameplay, persistent progression, and the player-driven conflicts of the EVE universe.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **PvPvE Deployments** — Fight rival squads, NPC soldiers, drones, and environmental threats in high-stakes planetary operations.
- 🚁 **Extract Valuable Loot** — Raid crash sites, complete objectives, recover resources, and escape with your rewards.
- 🧬 **Persistent Warclone Progression** — Upgrade your Warclone, dropsuits, weapons, and abilities through persistent progression.
- 📜 **Blueprint-Based Gear** — Collect blueprints to manufacture and customize equipment, weapons, dropsuits, and future personal drones.
- ⚔️ **Faction Campaigns** — Align with factions, complete objectives, and influence seasonal conflicts across New Eden.
- 🌌 **Connected EVE Universe** — Deployments contribute to a shared living universe connected to EVE Online.
- 🤝 **Solo or Squad Play** — Take on operations alone or deploy in squads of up to three players.
